## Posting tips

- **Video first:** native video gets far more reach than a link. Lead with it and put the link in a comment or your bio.
- **The first 2 seconds matter:** both videos open on the hero and are moving within the first second.
- **Timing:** for LinkedIn, Tuesday–Thursday mornings in your clients' time zone tend to work best.
- **Reply to every comment in the first hour.** It strongly boosts reach on LinkedIn and Instagram.
- **Pin it:** add the post to your LinkedIn "Featured" section and pin the Reel on Instagram.
